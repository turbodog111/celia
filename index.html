The recipe worked qualitatively: where C0.1/Mini-C0.1 produced multilingual gibberish, Mini-C0.2 is grammatically clean, takes turns, stops cleanly on <eos>, and carries real Christian vocabulary. Deliberately wide and shallow (768×7) to fit strict 30:1 in the overnight window — and it worked: 56.5k tok/s at 68M, matching the 50M Mini despite 37% more parameters, with lower VRAM. Lowest loss of any Celia model, and its open prose reads slightly cleaner than Mini’s — but its lower 15% Christian share shows up as weaker scripture mechanics (CSMB 12.0 vs Mini’s 24.0). Two lessons for the next model: keep concentration up, and (per MobileLLM) flip wide-shallow → deep-thin for quality.
