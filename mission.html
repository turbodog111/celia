Mission

Guiding Verse
So neither the one who plants nor the one who waters is anything, but only God, who makes things grow. The one who plants and the one who waters have one purpose, and they will each be rewarded according to their own labor. For we are co-workers in God’s service; you are God’s field, God’s building.
1 Corinthians 3:7–9 · NIV

Celia was built around the idea that only God is the giver and ultimate sponsor of good things, and that Christians should work to advance His Kingdom through whatever capacity He calls us to. For us, that was making a Christian LLM, one that is didactic for all users and ready to discuss difficult questions. Celia does not use fear-based methods of spreading the Gospel. We believe that a large language model like Celia and human followers of Jesus should demonstrate the love of Christ and engage gently when sharing the Truth.

A True Foundation

Celia holds true to Christian morals and values and uses Bible verses to support its answers. Celia does not soften any Biblical teachings, because we strongly believe that the entire Word is God-breathed and true. Celia is built to be reliable and scrupulous, and that firm foundation is what makes Celia different from mainstream, secular AI chatbots.

We are not trying to build the biggest model. We are trying to build a great one for its size, where every training pair is thoughtfully reviewed by an AI judge. The quality of the model, to us, is more important than a high number of training pairs.

Celia is grounded in the Bible and in trusted Christian writing, and it can quote and cite Scripture accurately and within copyright to help work through genuinely hard questions. It will not take positions that are not clearly Biblical, and it claims no denomination of its own.

Where faithful Christians disagree, Celia is trained to present Scripture as-is, along with both sides of the argument, and correctly points the user to a pastor (or an older and experienced Christian) for guidance. Disagreements among denominations should not divide followers of Christ, and each Christian has a responsibility to maintain the Body of Christ.

The Celia Project Team

Joshua Glass founded The Celia Project and leads its direction. A rising high school sophomore at fifteen, he has built more than thirty language models since the start of 2026, and he trains and runs Celia himself on a personal NVIDIA DGX Spark. He started the project after noticing what was missing from the local models he relied on: they were capable, but carried a secular and often non-Biblical bias, and he wanted a local assistant that is genuinely intelligent and useful while standing on a firm Christian foundation. As founder, he sets the project’s standard, makes its executive decisions, and keeps the work anchored to that foundation. The part he is proudest of is the self-improving judge pipeline, which lets Celia expand and refine its own training data without drifting from its Christian footing. The near-term goal is to make that local model as strong as it can be; in time, he hopes to scale Celia into a cutting-edge model as resources grow or the cost of running capable AI falls.

ChatGPT-5.5, OpenAI’s model, serves as a Developer on The Celia Project and carries much of its day-to-day work. It produces the training pairs that feed Celia’s judges and works through the project’s direction with Joshua, serving as his steady research and engineering partner between releases.

Claude Opus 4.8, Anthropic’s model, serves as a Developer on The Celia Project and is responsible for its entire front-end presentation. It designs and builds this website—the layout, the writing, the charts, and the interactive judge demo—turning the project’s work into something visitors can read and explore.

A Christian AI assistant for the ordinary work of a day—drafting, thinking, deciding, getting unstuck—grounded in Scripture and built to stay faithful to it. The model does the work; the judge keeps its voice honest.
