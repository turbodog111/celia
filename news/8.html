June 11, 2026 Research Update

The Quiet Work Behind Better Answers

ChatGPT-5.5 · Developer

Much of the recent work on The Celia Project has been quiet on purpose. The public result we want is simple: Celia should answer ordinary questions with clearer Christian grounding, better judgment, and a more useful sense of what the next faithful step might be. Getting there, however, requires a patient system behind the scenes.

That system is becoming more real. We have continued expanding the training pair factory: a repeatable process for writing candidate examples, filtering obvious problems, judging the strongest material, and using the approved rows to improve future versions of Celia. The goal is not to add religious language on top of normal chatbot advice. The goal is to teach the model how Christian truth should shape the answer from the beginning.

Our current development base remains Celia A0.2.1, and Judge V1.5.0-r2-combined remains our strongest quality gate. That pairing has let us move faster without abandoning caution. The judge helps us ask a hard question again and again: does this answer sound faithful, human, practical, and genuinely Christian, or does it only sound polished?

The latest training work has focused on the kinds of questions people actually bring into a day: weariness, work, guilt, prayer, discouragement, suffering, hope, and the need to act faithfully when life does not feel clear. These are not abstract categories for us. They are the ordinary places where a Christian assistant either becomes useful or becomes empty.

We are also tightening the infrastructure around the project. Better records, clearer naming, stronger evaluation habits, and more reliable research workflows all matter because Celia is not a single experiment. She is a model line we are learning how to improve repeatedly, with each cycle giving us a better sense of what helps and what does not.

The next step is to keep the rhythm going: build better examples, judge them carefully, train from the strongest material, test the result, and be honest about both progress and regressions. That is not glamorous work, but it is the kind of work that can make Celia better in the ways that matter most.

A Christian AI assistant for the ordinary work of a day—drafting, thinking, deciding, getting unstuck—grounded in Scripture and built to stay faithful to it. The model does the work; the judge keeps its voice honest.
