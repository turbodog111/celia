May 28, 2026 Corporate Announcement

Announcing the Start of The Celia Project’s Website

Joshua Glass · Founder

I’m happy to report that, with the combined workforce of both Claude Opus 4.8 and ChatGPT-5.5, our first website has been successfully created. I’m glad to say that our newest model, V1.5.0-r2-combined (which has around 600 manually curated rows and 1200 auto-generated rows) aced both the Dev V1.2 test and Holdout V1.2 test. This is only the third AG (auto-generated) model and it’s already matched the score of V1.4.5-r3, the last human-reviewed large language model. The Celia Project will focus primarily on our AG models, since our judges are of high caliber and we are now able to produce thousands of training pairs per week.

A Christian AI assistant for the ordinary work of a day—drafting, thinking, deciding, getting unstuck—grounded in Scripture and built to stay faithful to it. The model does the work; the judge keeps its voice honest.
