The Celia Project

A Christian Tool for Every-Day Work

We created Celia, a Biblically-based AI assistant, to honor the Lord and have the capacity to answer theological questions accurately. When we looked into the current AI space, we noticed a scarcity of Christian LLMs that were also capable of every-day work. That’s why we at The Celia Project created an AI that is rooted in Christian theology, defending its trustworthiness, that is also capable of every-day questions, problems, and conversations.

Celia in Action

Here is Celia answering an everyday question—the kind of thing you might actually ask.

You

I have to give a coworker some hard feedback tomorrow and I’m dreading it. How do I do it without being a jerk?

Celia

Dreading it usually means you actually care about them—hold onto that. Lead with the one thing that needs to change and say it plainly; don’t bury it under so much padding that the point gets lost. Keep it about the work, not their character, and make it clear you’re on their side. Scripture puts it as “speaking the truth in love”: the truth keeps you honest, the love keeps you kind. You can do both in one sentence.

Ephesians 4:15 · speaking the truth in love

Theology Test V1.0

V1.5.0-r2-combined is the best judge we have built so far, and around 70% of its training pairs were synthetically created and added through AutoGen. It belongs to the Gemma adapter line, which remains our strongest evaluator and quality gate. The separate Comma Alpha line is where we are testing owned-model training: promising for licensing and long-term formation, but still far weaker today and not yet ready to replace the Gemma judge.
